South Bound Trails, Adventure Package - 7 days & 6 Nights

Tour Details

Overview

This package condenses the very best of southern Sri Lanka by seamlessly blending wild land treks, ocean safaris, and rich cultural heritage into one complete journey. Embark on the ultimate thrill across Sri Lanka’s Southern Province with Kanneliya rainforest hikes, Galle river canoeing, and Unawatuna mountain biking.

 

Thi package includes dive into deep-sea fishing, Madolduwa boat rides, and offshore dolphin and whale watching along the vibrant coast. Encounter iconic landmarks including the roaring Hummanaya Blow Hole, traditional Thalpe stilt fishermen, and a historic Galle Dutch Fort walkthrough.

Day 1

Kanneliya Rainforest Hike

Kanneliya is a UNESCO biodiverse rainforest in Sri Lanka, famous for hiking, waterfalls, bird watching and wildlife.

Day 2

Fishing Tour - Galle

Set sail with local fishermen for an exciting deep-sea or lagoon fishing experience in Galle. Try traditional techniques, enjoy stunning ocean views, and even cook your fresh catch.

Day 2

River Safari & Canoeing Excursion in Galle

Paddle through lush mangroves, spot wildlife, and experience serene waters near Galle. A perfect blend of nature, adventure, and relaxation.

Day 3

Mountain Bike Tour Unawatuna

Cycle through lush trails, villages, and coastal viewpoints, exploring nature and local culture on an exciting off-road adventure.

Day 4

Madolduwa Boat Ride

Enjoy a scenic boat ride on Koggala Lake to Madol Duwa Island, famous for its lush mangroves, amd rich birdlife. A perfect mix of nature and adventure.

Day 4

Scuba Diving

Dive into Unawatuna's crystal-clear waters, exploring vibrant coral reefs, diverse marine life, and historic shipwrecks. Perfect for beginners and experienced divers, offering an unforgettable underwater adventure.

Day 5

Stits Fisherman Thalpe

A traditional Sri Lankan fishing method, where fishermen balance on wooden stilts to catch fish—a unique cultural sight.

Day 5

Hummanaya Blow Hole, Dickwella

Sri Lanka’s only blowhole and the second-largest in the world, Hummanaya shoots water up to 30 meters high, creating a spectacular natural display.

Day 6

Dolphine & Whale Watching

Mirissa is Sri Lanka’s top whale-watching and Dolphine- watching destination, offering sightings of blue whales, sperm whales, and dolphins in their natural habitat.

Day 7

Galle Dutch fort walkthrough

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Galle Dutch Fort is a historic colonial fortress built by the Portuguese in 1588 and later fortified by the Dutch. It features cobblestone streets, colonial architecture, museums, cafes, and stunning ocean views.
