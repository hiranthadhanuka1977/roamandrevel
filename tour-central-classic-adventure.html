Central Classic, Adventure Package - 7 days & 6 Nights

Tour Details

Overview

"Central Classic" adventure package is an journey through Sri Lanka's heartland. The tour takes you from the cityscapes of Colombo to the wilds of the central province. Highlights include a thrilling cycle tour of the Balanagala battlefield and adventurous hikes in the Knuckles, Hanthana, or Hunnasgiri mountain ranges. You'll also have the opportunity to canoe on the Mahaweli River and climb the iconic Pidurangala Rock. The tour concludes with an exhilarating wildlife safari at either Minneriya or Wasgamuwa National Park.

Day 1

National Museum of Colombo

Immerse yourself in Sri Lanka's rich cultural and historical heritage at the National Museum of Colombo, the largest and oldest museum in the country.

Day 1

Gangaramaya temple

Discover the unique blend of Sri Lankan and international architecture at this serene buddhist temple on Beira Lake.

Day 1

Independence Memorial hall

Visit the monument commemorating Sri Lanka's independence, designed after the Kandyan Royal Audience Hall.

Day 1

Lotus Tower

Ascend the iconic Lotus Tower for breathtaking panoramic views of Colombo.

Day 1

Galle Face green and One galle face Mall

Experience the contrast of a seaside promenade and modern luxury shopping in one location.

Day 1

Dinner by the Mount Lavinia beach

Enjoy a magical dinner on the beach with the perfect backdrop of a Sri Lankan sunset.

Day 2

St Anthoneys Church - Kotahena

Visit this national shrine, revered for its peaceful atmosphere and miraculous powers.

Day 2

Colombo Dutch Museum

Step back in time at this preserved 17th-century building to see Dutch colonial artifacts.

Day 2

Dutch Hospital shopping

Explore a beautifully restored 17th-century hospital now home to trendy shops and restaurants.

Day 2

Colombo Port City

Discover this new urban development on reclaimed land, featuring luxury hubs and South Asia's first downtown duty-free retail destination.

Day 2

City of Dreams

"Cinnamon Life City of Dreams" is a massive integrated resort in Colombo, featuring luxury hotels, a shopping mall, and entertainment venues.

Day 2

Diyatha Uyana and Dinner at Waters Edge Hotel

Explore the scenic Diyatha Uyana park on the shores of Diyawanna Oya, and conclude your evening with a delightful dinner at the elegant Waters Edge hotel.

Day 3

Pinnawala Elephant Opharnage

Visit the Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage to witness the world's largest herd of captive elephants, where you can watch them feed and bathe in the river.

Day 3

Kandy shopping

Explore Kandy's shopping scene, from the modern Kandy City Centre mall to the bustling Main Market, offering a wide range of local handicrafts, spices, tea, and souvenirs.

Day 3

Balanagala battle feild Cycle tour

Embark on an exhilarating cycling tour through the historic Balana battlefield, where you'll pedal through scenic villages and tea plantations while learning about Sri Lanka's past.

Day 4

Dalada Maligawa (Temple of the Tooth Relic)

Visit the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic in Kandy, a UNESCO World Heritage site. It houses a tooth relic of the Buddha and is one of the most revered Buddhist temples in the world.

Day 4

Ambuluwawa

Climb the iconic tower at Ambuluwawa for incredible 360-degree views. It's a unique multi-religious center with a temple, kovil, mosque, and church.

Day 5

Knuckeles/ Hanthana/ Hunnasgiri Mountain range hike

Trek through the misty, rugged landscapes of the Knuckles, Hanthana, or Hunnasgiriya mountain ranges, offering stunning views and a chance to explore rich biodiversity.

Day 6

Canoeing in Mahaweli river

Go canoeing on the majestic Mahaweli River, Sri Lanka's longest river, to enjoy a unique perspective on the country's lush wilderness, birdlife, and serene landscapes.

Day 6

Dambulla Royal cave temple

Explore the UNESCO World Heritage site of the Dambulla Royal Cave Temple, a stunning complex of five caves filled with over 150 Buddha statues and intricate murals.

Day 7

Sigiriya

Climb the magnificent Sigiriya Rock Fortress, a UNESCO World Heritage site known as "Lion Rock." This ancient citadel, with its stunning frescoes and water gardens, is a testament to Sri Lankan engineering and art.

Day 7

Minneriya/ Wasgamuwa National Park Safari

Enjoy a jeep safari at either Minneriya or Wasgamuwa National Park, both famous for their large elephant herds and diverse wildlife.

Day 7

Pidurangala Rock Hike

Hike up Pidurangala Rock for an alternative perspective of Sigiriya, offering stunning panoramic views of the Lion Rock and the surrounding landscape, especially at sunrise or sunset.
