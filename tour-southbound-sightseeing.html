South Bound Trails, Sightseeing Package - 7 days & 6 Nights

Tour Details

Overview

Embark on a seven-day journey to the stunning islands of the Maldives. Relax on white-sand beaches, swim in turquoise waters, and indulge in luxurious resort stays. This package is perfect for couples and families seeking a serene tropical getaway.

Day 1

Ridiyagama Safari

Explore Sri Lanka’s first open-air safari, spotting lions, tigers, Zebra, camels, bird species and more on an exciting jeep adventure.

Day 2

Birds Park & Birds Research Center

A dedicated facility aimed at the conservation, research, and exhibition of birds, particularly focusing on species found in Sri Lanka and those that migrate through the region.

Day 2

Bundala Safari

A UNESCO biosphere reserve, is a birdwatcher’s paradise, home to migratory flamingos, elephants, crocodiles, and diverse wildlife. Explore its lagoons, wetlands, and grasslands on an exciting jeep safari.

Day 3

Madunagala Hot Water Spring

A natural hot water spring near Ridiyagama, known for its healing mineral-rich waters.

Day 4

Yala Safari

Sri Lanka’s most famous wildlife park, Yala offers thrilling jeep safaris to spot leopards, elephants, sloth bears, and diverse bird species. Its lush forests, grasslands, and lagoons make it a top destination for wildlife enthusiasts

Day 5

Madolduwa Boat Ride

Enjoy a scenic boat ride on Koggala Lake to Madol Duwa Island, famous for its lush mangroves, and rich birdlife. A perfect mix of nature and adventure

Day 6

Sea Turtle Hatchery Centre, Mahamodara

A conservation center dedicated to protecting sea turtles, where visitors can see hatchlings, injured turtles, and various turtle species.

Day 6

Japanese peace pagoda Rumassala

A serene Buddhist stupa on a Hill, offering panoramic ocean views and a peaceful atmosphere.

Day 6

Jungle Beach

A hidden paradise near Unawatuna, Jungle Beach offers calm turquoise waters, soft golden sand, and lush greenery. Perfect for swimming, snorkeling, and a peaceful getaway away from the crowds.

Day 7

Galle Dutch fort

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Galle Dutch Fort is a historic colonial fortress built by the Portuguese in 1588 and later fortified by the Dutch. It features cobblestone streets, colonial architecture, museums, cafes, and stunning ocean views...
